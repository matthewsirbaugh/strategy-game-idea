# Look references

Frames Bryson chose for the environment's look (2026-09-29). What each one is for is recorded in
[the style study](../../../explorations/environment-style-study.md).

| File | Source | What to take from it |
|---|---|---|
| `edgerunners-afterlife-bar.webp` | Cyberpunk: Edgerunners, the Afterlife bar | The target. Thin dark lines on architecture, lower in contrast than the characters'. Flat panel fills with soft mottled grime inside each panel. One light color floods the scene. Neon as flat bright bands with a glow |
| `cp2077-courtyard-treehouse-ninjas.webp` | Cyberpunk 2077, environment art by Treehouse Ninjas | Detail from structure: catwalks, railings, cables, doors. Calm cracked asphalt with one lit puddle. Photoreal, so take the density, not the surfaces |
| `neon-street-render.webp` | 3D neon street render, artist unknown | Dark neutral buildings, all color from signs, wet road repeating them as vertical streaks, lane and crosswalk paint on the ground |
| `cp2077-daytime-street.webp` | Cyberpunk 2077 gameplay | Ads, murals and stripe bands as the wall texture on plain dark faces; graffiti at street level; painted curbs |
| `sadlos-city-concept.webp` | Concept art by Artur Sadlos | Layered depth: dark silhouettes in front, glowing city behind, haze between. For the skyline beyond the map |
