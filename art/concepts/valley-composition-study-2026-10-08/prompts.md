## Shared specification

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: wide landscape environment art-direction study for an original near-future strategy role-playing game.
Input image: the supplied image is a THEME AND WORLD REFERENCE ONLY. Preserve its recognizable modern American setting, pristine OpenBrain corporate contrast, grounded advanced devices and resistance computing packs. Completely redesign the site plan, building silhouettes, landscaping and camera composition as described below. Do not trace the reference intersection, balcony foreground, New York-style brick/fire-escape neighborhood, or square tower composition.
Primary request: reimagine this theme with a believable California Silicon Valley character: predominantly current-day modern architecture with plausible near-future technology everywhere. Society is dominated by AI corporations. Ordinary residents survive on universal basic income and compete for scarce manual work. Human resistance Operators carry advanced open-source AI inside practical mobile computing backpacks; their AI can control cameras, access doors, autonomous vehicles and other networked real-world devices.
World and architecture: recognizable Northern California city or valley town, low and midrise mixed-use development, broad modern streets, some stucco and ordinary glass office buildings, mature oaks, occasional palms, dry hills in the distance, drought-tolerant planted verges, bicycles and real cars. The surrounding city is reasonably maintained but financially constrained and unevenly upgraded, rather than an abandoned slum. OpenBrain alone has immaculate white/pale-ivory architectural surfaces, inky black glazing, meticulous landscaping and sparse precise red security accents. Strong territory and wealth contrast through materials, architectural discipline, lighting and maintenance. Advanced tech is visible and integrated into normal urban life: realistic small autonomous delivery vehicles, sensor-equipped street furniture, surveillance cameras, badge-controlled doors, illuminated gate readers, charging bays and discreet utility equipment.
People and devices: include two human Operators as small-to-medium figures integrated into the scene, with visible rectangular cream/black vented computing backpacks, wear and a small cyan status strip; practical contemporary clothing, adult proportions. They should help establish scale and the AI partnership, without occupying a quarter of the picture. Corporate personnel wear white and black with minute red accents. A camera and door reader with small cyan lights can quietly suggest resistance control. A modest benefits kiosk or work-lottery terminal and a few residents can hint at the economy; keep them secondary.
Lighting and atmosphere: dim California blue hour or late dusk with restrained warm inhabited windows, clean white corporate architectural lights and tiny red security details. Beautiful controlled lighting, clear atmospheric depth, readable open ground. Color from realistic light and surfaces, with little ornamental neon. City palette has natural warm greys, sandy stucco, muted slate and olive vegetation; OpenBrain stands out in white, black and small red accents.
Text (verbatim): a restrained architectural wordmark "OPENBRAIN". A distant small office building may say "ANTHROPOMORPHIC". Secondary civic terminals may say "BENEFITS" or "WORK LOTTERY". All writing in English, no dominant slogans.
Finish and realism: produce a clean, refined and plausible image with realistic scale, perspective and believable materials. Crisp silhouettes and designed surfaces, nuanced smooth shading. Greatly reduce the source image's oily brush texture, heavy grunge, scratches everywhere, rain-soaked road and sludge. Weathering is light and selective; modern city and pristine corp should have an immediately clear surface difference. Dry or mildly damp ground instead of a mirror-wet street. No thick paint marks or canvas grain. Human faces and anatomy remain realistic; avoid chibi or heavily cartooned people.
Output and constraints: one full-bleed approximately 16:9 landscape image, one composition, no panels, no title, no border, no watermark, no game interface or grid. This is a separate environment option, not a crop or recoloring of the reference. Change architectural silhouette, spatial organization, landscaping and viewpoint decisively.
Avoid: dense Manhattan or East Asian cyberpunk megacity shorthand, continuous rain and fog, exaggerated poverty grime, giant holograms, magic network beams, robots replacing the human Operators, gratuitous mechs, fantastical buildings, uniform dystopian brutalism, skyscraper forests, oversaturated neon trim, photoreal microtexture noise, an identical foreground balcony with two huge backs.
