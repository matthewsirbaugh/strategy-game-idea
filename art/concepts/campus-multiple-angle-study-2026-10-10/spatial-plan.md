# Shared site relationships for the campus study

This is a production convention inferred from Bryson's selected
[mountainside night overview](../rear-research-park-study-2026-10-10/01-rear-campus-overview-v3-hillside-night-robot-dogs.png).
North means uphill here. These are relative orientation labels for consistent image prompts,
not surveyed geographic bearings, adopted building footprints or level-design rules.

| Anchor | Shared location |
|---|---|
| Main headquarters and long glazed front gallery | Highest northern terrace, with the rocky mountain behind it |
| Privacy wall and dark service gate | Lower southern boundary, gate slightly east of the central garden |
| City | Outside the lower boundary and below the campus, spreading across the south/west valley |
| Western lab pavilion and autonomous-shuttle court | West of the central garden |
| Water garden | Center of the terraced park |
| Two branching white solar canopies | West bank of the water garden |
| Greenhouse and instrumented growing beds | East/southeast of the garden |
| Upper eastern lab terraces | Above/behind the greenhouse |

The cameras change the view without moving those anchors:

| View | Camera and direction | Main building | West lab / vehicle court | Greenhouse | Solar canopies / water |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Inside gate | Lower south, facing north/slightly northwest | Uphill background | Left | Right | Canopies left/west of water |
| 2. Water garden | East bank, facing northwest | Uphill/right | Left/middle, beyond canopies | Behind camera | Canopies across the water on its west bank |
| 3. Inside upper gallery | Main building's north gallery, facing south | Camera is inside it | Outdoor right | Outdoor left | Canopies right/west of water |
| 4. Overhead roof plan | Above the site, uphill/north at top | Top of frame | Left | Lower right | Canopies left/west of water |

The south gate and most of the downhill city are behind the first two cameras; a far western
city glimpse can appear at the edge of the northwest-facing view. In the third view the gate
and city are downhill beyond the gardens. The main headquarters must not reappear as a second
building in front of that camera. A south-facing view reverses screen left/right, while
geographic west/east stay fixed.

The selected overview is Image 1 in every generation. Completed closer views are supplied as
supporting references to later views after inspection, preserving recognizable architectural
landmarks. Original landscape and architecture are the authority when a closer interpretation
is ambiguous.

Lighting stays at the selected readable nighttime exposure: largely dark office windows and
unlit path stretches, with cool shadow detail and selective small warm pools at thresholds.
People remain sparse. Any dog is a compact mechanical quadruped matching the corrected
white/black/red patrol design.

Visual review checks landmark side/order, elevation, viewing direction, material family,
night lighting, security hardware and robot morphology. Generated illustrations cannot
verify exact dimensions or visibility/pathfinding; those require a future modeled site.
