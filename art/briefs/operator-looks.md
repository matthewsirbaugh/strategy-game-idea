# Briefs: the Operators' looks, first concepts

Concept images of the three Operators, to choose a look for each (Bryson, 2026-10-12). These are
exploratory: they go in `art/concepts/`, not `art/sources/`, and nobody models from them yet. The
character models exploration is still parked. Who the three are is in
[the story exploration](../../explorations/core-idea-and-story.md#team-2026-10-12).

## What every image needs

- **One person, full body, head to boots,** standing in a relaxed neutral pose, arms slightly away
  from the body so the silhouette reads. Plain mid-grey background, soft even light, no text.
- **Masked.** Faces are covered in V1: everyone in this world can be identified from one photo.
  Hair, build and hands still show.
- **The backpack is part of the character.** It houses their AI partner, a local AGI: a compact
  computer about the size of a small hiking backpack, with heat vents and cable runs. There is no
  screen, face or glowing eyes; the AI has no appearance. Each pack looks like its owner built or
  bought it.
- **Three views on one sheet:** front, back (showing the pack), and a small view from above at about
  50 degrees, the battle camera's angle. In the game a person is about 55 pixels tall, so the
  silhouette has to tell the three apart.
- **The world:** a poor California city in 2046, visually close to today. Realistic, grounded,
  nothing futuristic beyond the pack. Worn, lived-in clothes. The enemy, OpenBrain, is white with
  black and red accents, so the Operators avoid that palette. No team colour yet.
- **Open, for Astra to propose:** ethnicity, hair, skin tone and exact masks where the brief doesn't
  set them. Keep each person the same across their looks so Bryson compares clothes, not people.

Save every image in `art/concepts/operator-looks-2026-10-12/`, named after the look below.

## The tradesman, the protagonist (he, early 30s)

Works at a data center; his father owns a small electrical subcontractor, which makes him the
team's most privileged member. Three looks:

1. `tradesman-workwear`: heritage workwear. A canvas chore coat, duck work pants, leather work
   boots, a few tool-belt pieces. His father's company patch on the coat, taped over. Mask: a
   half-face respirator and tinted safety glasses.
2. `tradesman-hivis`: hi-vis utility. Safety orange or lime with reflective strips, a hard hat
   clipped to his pack. Mask: a half-face respirator and clear safety glasses.
3. `tradesman-quiet-money`: quiet money, faded. Good basics, well cut, a little worn: the rich kid
   trying not to look rich. Mask: a plain matte black face mask and dark glasses under a cap.

His pack: well built, neat cable runs, his own wiring.

## The writer (she, early 30s)

Her journalism degree became useless the moment she finished it; she writes the resistance's
illegal newspapers. Her signature: large round glasses with flip-up IR lenses, and a bandana she
pulls over her face when she needs to. Two looks:

1. `writer-printer`: anti-surveillance mixed with the ink-stained printer. A work apron or shop coat
   over plain clothes, rolled sleeves, ink on her hands and cuffs, a deep hood. No face paint. The
   round glasses, bandana pulled up.
2. `writer-reporter`: field reporter mixed with thrifted layers. A many-pocketed vest over
   mismatched secondhand layers, a long coat, scarves, practical boots, notebook and pencil. The
   round glasses, bandana pulled up.

Her pack: small, battered and patched, papers and zines tucked in the straps.

## The developer (he, mid-40s)

A former senior software engineer, let go with the last of them when AGI reached enterprise; the
only one who remembers working before AI. One look:

1. `developer-dad-hacker`: the dad mixed with the old-school hacker. Heavyset, a round friendly
   face, a short beard with some grey, glasses. A black tee and a worn leather jacket, cargo pants,
   sensible shoes. Warm and unflashy, the oldest presence on the team. Mask: a dark neck gaiter
   pulled up over his nose, glasses over it.

His pack: overbuilt and custom, the most sophisticated rig on the team, old stickers on it.
