## For Astra

Everything in [the proof run's "What every image needs"](proof-run.md#what-every-image-needs-for-meshy)
applies. Each prompt is the proof run's style block, then the object below, then its framing block.
Main view only. Attach the finished images in `art/sources/` for the shared design language, plus
the reference each item names, for design only. Save Bryson's chosen image as
`art/sources/props/<id>/01-main.png`.

## 1. Ground uplight: `props/uplight`

- **In the world:** the warm lights set into the ground that wash the campus's white walls and
  trees from below ([terrace](../reference/openbrain-research-park-terrace.png)).
- **Size:** about 25 cm across, 12 cm tall.

> The object: an in-ground architectural uplight for a corporate campus, shown with the part that
> sits above the paving. About 25 cm across. A round black anodized aluminum rim around a
> frosted glass lens glowing soft warm white, with a short black housing below it. Small, simple
> and precise.

## 2. Solar panel rack: `props/solar_rack`

- **In the world:** the rows of solar panels on the park's roofs
  ([overhead](../reference/openbrain-campus-overhead.png)). Claude lines them up in rows.
- **Size:** about 3.0 m wide, 1.5 m deep, 1.0 m tall at its high edge, to fit the grid.

> The object: one rooftop solar panel rack for a modern research building: a row of three dark
> blue-black solar panels side by side, tilted toward the viewer at about 20 degrees, on a low
> black aluminum frame with short feet. About 3 metres wide. Thin silver panel edges, clean and
> new, no cables showing.

## 3. Security turnstile: `props/turnstile`

- **In the world:** the face-scanning turnstiles at the campus's pedestrian checkpoints
  ([threshold](../reference/openbrain-front-security-threshold.png)).
- **In the game:** a barrier at checkpoints for now; it may become a device later.
- **Size:** about 1.0 m tall, 1.4 m long, 0.3 m wide.

> The object: a single modern optical security turnstile lane barrier at a corporate campus
> entrance. About 1 metre tall and 1.4 metres long. A slim white pedestal with chamfered edges,
> a black glass top surface, a small face-scanning camera with a soft warm white ring on a short
> black stalk at its entry end, two clear glass swing wings at its middle, closed, and a thin red
> light line along its top edge.

## 4. Robot dog dock: `props/robot_dock`

- **In the world:** where the campus's security robot dogs park and charge between patrols
  ([river crossing](../reference/openbrain-research-park-river-crossing.png) shows the dogs).
- **Size:** about 1.2 m wide, 0.9 m deep, 1.1 m tall.

> The object: a charging dock for a four-legged security robot, empty, on a corporate campus.
> About 1.2 metres wide. A low white platform with chamfered edges and a black rubber floor pad,
> with a white curved back panel rising behind it holding a black charging arm, a row of small
> warm white status lights, and one red light. Clean and new. No robot in it.
