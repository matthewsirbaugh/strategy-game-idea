## The slice, in short

A corner of OpenBrain's research park at night. A two-storey lab building, about 12 m by 9 m.
Ground floor: a black-framed glass curtain wall, 1.5 m mullion spacing, a lit lab behind it with
benches and a robot arm. Upper floor: matte white composite panels, 1.5 m wide, with thin dark
seams and one long glazed band. A walkable roof terrace behind a 1.1 m parapet topped with glass
railing, with two rooftop air-handling units, a row of solar panels, a strip of green roof and
planters. A dark steel stair runs up the outside of one side, ground to roof, with a landing at the
upper floor, 1.5 m wide. A skybridge leaves the upper floor at the far corner: 3 m wide, one storey
tall, glass walls, black frame, diagonal bracing, lit warm inside. Light stone paving with warm
uplights washing the white walls, one tall pole light, a bench, wet ground after rain. Red appears
in very few places: one thin accent line and an indicator light or two.
