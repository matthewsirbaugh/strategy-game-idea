  lighting and plain blue-grey background.
- **The same layout:** front and back full body, masked; an unmasked head-and-shoulders portrait
  top right; the battle-camera view from above bottom right.

  no appearance.

## The writer (she, early 30s)

Black, deep brown skin, short natural curls, a lean build. Her journalism degree became useless the
moment she finished it; she writes the resistance's illegal newspapers.

- **Signature:** large round glasses with flip-up IR lenses, and a bandana she pulls over her face.
  Masked in the front and back views, bandana down in the portrait.
- **Outfit:** a fitted hooded jacket or shop coat, sleeves pushed up, dark trousers and boots. A
  smudge of ink on her fingers and cuffs is the only nod to the printing.
- **Her colour: magenta**, printer's ink, on the bandana and a few accents.
- **Her pack:** smaller and lighter than his, patched, a folded paper tucked under a strap.

Save as `art/concepts/operator-sheets-2026-10-13/writer.png`.

## The developer (he, mid-40s)

Light skin, a heavyset build, a round friendly face, a short beard with some grey, glasses. A
former senior software engineer, let go with the last of them; the only one of the three who
remembers working before AI. Warm and unflashy, the oldest presence on the team.

- **Outfit:** the dad mixed with the old-school hacker. A worn leather jacket over a black tee,
  dark cargo pants, sensible boots.
- **Mask:** a neck gaiter pulled up over his nose, glasses over it. Gaiter down in the portrait.
- **His colour: terminal green**, like an old phosphor screen, on the gaiter and a few accents.
- **His pack:** the most overbuilt rig on the team, latches and heat-sink fins, old stickers.

Save as `art/concepts/operator-sheets-2026-10-13/developer.png`.
