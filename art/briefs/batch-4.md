## 1. Turret, revised: `devices/turret`

The first turret ([batch 2's candidate](../concepts/batch-2-device-review-2026-10-11/01-turret-main.png))
reused the camera head's housing, so from the battle camera it read as a camera with a gun. A
camera and a turret ask the player for opposite things, so they must never be confused (Bryson
agreed, 2026-10-11). Its pedestal, materials and quality were right; the head needs a silhouette
no camera has. Don't attach the camera head or the first turret as references for this one.

- **In the game:** an autonomous turret. It targets the nearest enemy by itself, or holds fire;
  the AI can take it over.
- **Size:** about 1.3 m tall, the head about 0.8 m wide.
- **Must read from the battle camera:** a weapon, and which way it faces, at a glance.

> The object: an autonomous security turret on a corporate research campus. About 1.3 metres
> tall. A low, wide, crouched armored head in matte white composite with sharp chamfered planes,
> wider than it is long, like a helmet seen from the side, carrying two short black barrels side
> by side at its front. Instead of a camera lens, one thin horizontal black glass sensor slit
> across the head's front, glowing faintly red. The head sits on a heavy black anodized aluminum
> turntable on a short, wide black pedestal with a flush base plate, as if bolted to paving.
> Premium and controlled, quietly predatory; not military camouflage, no ammunition belts, no
> round lenses, nothing that resembles a security camera.

- **Size:** about 1.8 m tall when raised, on a 0.6 m square base.

> The object: a six-axis industrial research robot arm, raised in a poised working pose, on its
