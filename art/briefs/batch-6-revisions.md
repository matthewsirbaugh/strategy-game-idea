## 1. EV charger, revised: `props/ev_charger`

The first charger ([batch 3's candidate](../concepts/batch-3-review-2026-10-11/06-ev-charger-main.png))
was well made, but it's a white pillar about 1.4 m tall with a black glass front and the same
circular screen graphic as the access terminal. From the battle camera, decor must never pass for
the most-used device in the game (Bryson agreed, 2026-10-11). It needs a silhouette of its own:
low, dark, no screen. Don't attach the access terminal or the first charger as references.

- **In the world:** the chargers at the forecourt and the car park.
- **Size:** about 1.1 m tall; the post about 15 cm square, the charging box about 40 x 30 x 15 cm.

> The object: a compact electric vehicle charger for a corporate campus car park. About 1.1
> metres tall. A short, slim black anodized aluminum post with a flush base plate, carrying a
> rounded-rectangle charging box in matte graphite grey mounted on its top half, facing the
> viewer. The box has a single thin warm white status light line and a black charging connector
> holstered in a recess on its front, its thick black cable coiled once around a hook on the
> post's side. No screen, no display, no white panels, no red.
